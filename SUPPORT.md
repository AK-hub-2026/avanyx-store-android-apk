## 🛠️ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How do I build the project locally?
Please refer to our comprehensive setup guide in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### Where does the app load its catalog?
The AVANYX Store client loads real-time data directly from the official Firestore production database instance `ai-studio-avanyxstore-083c830c-ab08-4b8b-aaa3-6b723506e575` and caches items locally using Jetpack Room for fast offline browsing.

### How can I publish my app to AVANYX Store?
Apps published with status `PUBLISHED` in the AVANYX Firestore collection immediately sync down to all active Android clients in real-time.
